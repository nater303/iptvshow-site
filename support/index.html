IPTVSHOW Support

Help for the personal media player. Bring your own playlist and watch on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV & Mac.

Getting started

  • Open Settings → Playlists → Add New Playlist.
  • Paste your own M3U / M3U+ URL, an Xtream Codes portal link, or choose a local playlist file.
  • The app imports your Live TV, Movies and Series automatically.

IPTVSHOW does not include or provide any channels or content — you add your own legal playlist.

A channel won't play or keeps buffering

  • Try Settings → Video Player → Player → Enhanced for tougher codecs and stronger buffering.
  • Raise Buffer Size (e.g. 30s) and, on slow connections, cap the Max Bitrate.
  • Freezing usually comes from your provider's server or connection, not the app — a wired connection helps.

Subtitles / audio language

With the Enhanced player, open the on-screen player menu to choose subtitles/CC and audio tracks (when your stream provides them).

Change the app language

IPTVSHOW follows your device language automatically. To change it, change your device language in iOS/tvOS/macOS Settings → General → Language & Region.
