Privacy Policy

IPTVSHOW · Last updated: 2026

The short version

IPTVSHOW does not collect, track, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs. Your playlists and preferences stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • The playlists you add (M3U/Xtream URLs or files) and their credentials.
  • Your favorites, continue-watching history, and app settings.

If you enable iCloud, your playlists are synced privately through your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. This data is controlled by Apple's iCloud and is not accessible to us.

Network connections

The app connects directly to the playlist servers and streams that you add, in order to load and play your content. It does not send your data to us or to any third party. We do not operate, host, or control those servers.

Content responsibility

IPTVSHOW is a media player only. It does not provide, host, or endorse any channels or content. You are solely responsible for the playlists you add and for complying with the laws in your region.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. Because playlists can point to unfiltered internet content, a parental PIN is available in Settings.
